Your meetings stay on your Mac.

Plain-English privacy. No surprises, no fine-print gotchas. Last updated October 2026.

shield_lock
The short version: audio, transcripts, and summaries are processed and stored on your Mac by default. Nothing leaves your device unless you opt in to a cloud AI provider. We don't even know you're using the app.

What runs on your Mac

  • System-audio capture during supported calls (Teams, Zoom, Google Meet)
  • Whisper transcription (Apple Silicon, Neural Engine when available)
  • Speaker diarization (pyannote)
  • AI summarization when configured with a local LLM (Ollama, LM Studio)
  • Vector search across your own transcript archive

What leaves your Mac — and when

By default: nothing. If you choose to bring your own API key for cloud summarization, the prompt + transcript are sent to your chosen provider under their terms. Minutely does not proxy, log, or store these requests. You can flip the switch back at any time in Settings, and we'll never send anything unless you ask us to.

What we (the makers of Minutely) collect

  • A licence key check on first launch (verifies your key is valid and tied to your Mac)
  • No telemetry, no analytics, no "improvement" programs
  • Crash reports are opt-in, off by default, and never include meeting content

Your data, your control

Uninstall Minutely and everything we ever touched on your Mac is gone — recordings, transcripts, summaries, prompt presets, speaker registry, settings. The encrypted local store is wiped. There is no cloud backup to delete because we never had one.

Children's privacy

Minutely is a productivity tool for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we change anything material, we'll surface it in the app on next launch and ask you to re-acknowledge before continuing. We will never silently expand what we collect.

Contact

Questions? Email privacy@minutely.app — a real person responds within one business day.
