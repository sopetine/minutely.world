Everything you need to install Minutely, understand how it works, and fix the rare thing that goes wrong.

Getting started

Installation

Buy Minutely (or sign in if you already have it), open the .dmg, and drag it to Applications. On first launch macOS will ask for Screen Recording and Accessibility — grant both. Without them Minutely can't see the meeting or hear the audio.

Your first recording

Join any Teams, Zoom, or Google Meet call. Minutely detects it within a couple of seconds — the menu bar icon will turn solid red while the call is being recorded.

Features

Automatic recording

Minutely watches the window titles of Teams, Zoom, and Google Meet and starts recording the moment you join. No bot joins the call — the detection happens entirely on your Mac.

Transcription & speakers

Whisper runs locally to transcribe every recording. Language is detected on the first few seconds; speaker names are added when diarization is on so you know who said what.

Summaries & chapters

A short summary and chapter markers are written the moment your call ends. Open any past recording and press Summarize again to regenerate it with a different preset or your own AI.

Catalog, search & ask (RAG)

Every meeting is indexed automatically. Search by keyword, or ask in plain English — Minutely returns an answer plus the exact timestamp in the original recording.

Prompt presets & your own AI

Pick a preset summary style ("Stand-up", "1:1", "Discovery call"…), or paste any OpenAI-compatible API key in Settings to use your own model.

Local LLM support

Switch to a local model (Llama, Mistral, Phi) for fully offline summaries. Recommended on Apple Silicon Macs — Intel works too, just slower.

Privacy & data

Recordings, transcripts, and summaries stay on your Mac by default and are never uploaded anywhere. The only thing that leaves your device is what you explicitly send to a cloud AI provider you configure yourself — or it stays fully local with a local model.

What leaves your Mac: nothing by default. The only thing that ever leaves is the transcript text you send to a configured AI provider when you press Summarize.

Supported platforms

Microsoft Teams (including the new Teams 2.0), Zoom, and Google Meet. macOS 13 or later (Apple Silicon recommended).

  • Microsoft Teams — including the new Teams 2.0 client
  • Zoom — desktop app, browser, and breakout rooms
  • Google Meet — Chrome, Safari, and the standalone Meet app

Pricing & licensing

Shortcuts & menu bar

⌘ ⇧ R — start or stop a recording manually (works even when no meeting is active).

Menu bar icon states

  • Idle — outlined circle, no active recording
  • Recording — solid red filled circle, audio being captured
  • Processing — half-filled circle, transcription in progress

Click the menu bar icon to toggle recording, or right-click for the full context menu.

Troubleshooting

Minutely isn't detecting my meeting.

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and make sure Minutely is toggled on. Without this, Minutely can't read the title of the meeting window and won't know a call has started.

Transcription seems slow.

The first run downloads the Whisper model — later runs are faster. On Apple Silicon, switch to the small or medium model for the best balance of speed and accuracy. Large is accurate but ~3× slower.

Audio quality is poor.

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording and confirm Minutely is toggled on. Without it, the menu bar icon turns red but the recording is silent.

The summary didn't generate.

Open Settings → AI and confirm a provider is configured — either an OpenAI-compatible API key, or a loaded local model in the Models pane. If using a local model, wait for it to finish loading, then press Summarize again.
