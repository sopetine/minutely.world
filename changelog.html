v2.4.1 · Latest

Local LLM support

Oct 10, 2026

The most-requested feature since launch. Minutely now ships a first-class integration with Ollama and LM Studio. Pick a local model, set a base URL, and your summaries never leave the machine — even when the Wi-Fi cuts out.

  • Native Ollama and LM Studio discovery
  • Llama 3.1 8B, Mistral 7B, Phi-3 mini pre-tuned for meeting summaries
  • Zero-latency mode for offline meetings
  • Automatic fallback to your cloud key if the local model is unavailable
v2.3.0

Calendar-aware auto-arm

Sep 26, 2026

Minutely now reads your macOS Calendar (with permission) and arms itself the moment a Teams or Zoom meeting starts. No clicks, no popups.

  • EventKit integration for calendar detection
  • "Auto-arm for the next 2:00 PM" badge
  • Per-event "skip" toggle in the calendar

Speaker diarization

Every word in the transcript is now attributed to a named speaker. pyannote runs on-device, no audio ever uploaded.

Quickstart

  1. Download Minutely and drag it to Applications.
  2. Grant Microphone and Screen Recording on first launch.
  3. Optionally add your OpenAI / Anthropic / local-LLM key in Settings.
  4. Open Teams or Zoom. Minutely arms itself. The recording lands in ~/Movies/OBS/ as soon as you hang up.

That's it. No accounts to create, no servers to deploy.

About Minutely

Minutely is built by a four-person team in San Francisco. We're funded by customers, not VCs — every dollar you spend keeps the product local-only and subscription-free.

Why local-only? Because the most important conversations at work are the ones you don't want leaving the room.
