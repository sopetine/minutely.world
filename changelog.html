Changelog.

Minutely, as it's grown — every release since private beta, in plain English.

v1.4 Latest October 3, 2026

Teams workspaces & a fresh UI.

  • add_circleNew: Team workspaces with a shared meeting catalog and shared prompt library
  • auto_awesomeImproved: Faster transcription on Apple Silicon Macs
  • buildFixed: Memory leak when joining Zoom calls with screen sharing
v1.3 September 8, 2026

Local LLMs. Lower memory.

  • add_circleNew: Local LLM support — run summaries fully offline, no API key required
  • auto_awesomeImproved: Lower memory footprint during long recordings
  • buildFixed: Occasional duplicate speaker labels in long meetings
v1.2 August 11, 2026

Prompt presets & bring your own AI.

  • add_circleNew: Prompt presets — switch summary style with one click
  • add_circleNew: Bring your own AI — connect any OpenAI-compatible API key
  • buildFixed: Summary generation failing on meetings longer than 2 hours
v1.1 July 7, 2026

The catalog & "ask your meetings".

  • add_circleNew: Meeting catalog — every past recording, searchable in one place
  • add_circleNew: "Ask your meetings" — natural-language Q&A across your transcript history (RAG)
  • auto_awesomeImproved: Diarisation accuracy for meetings with 5+ speakers
v1.0 Public launch June 2, 2026

Public launch.

  • add_circleNew: Google Meet support, joining Teams and Zoom
  • add_circleNew: Pricing tiers — Personal, Pro, Team, Enterprise
  • add_circleNew: One-click purchase, no subscription
v0.3 May 5, 2026

Summaries you can actually read. Auto-chapters land in the same pass.

  • add_circleNew: AI-generated summaries and auto chapters
  • auto_awesomeImproved: Faster language detection at the start of a call
v0.2 April 6, 2026

Zoom support, beta diarisation.

  • add_circleNew: Zoom support
  • add_circleNew: Speaker diarisation (beta) — know who said what
  • buildFixed: Recording occasionally not stopping when a meeting ended early
