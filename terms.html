Your license

A Minutely license is a one-time purchase that grants you the right to use the current and all future versions of Minutely on the number of Macs specified by the tier you bought.

  • Personal ($49): 1 Mac, 1 user.
  • Pro ($99): 1 Mac, 1 user.
  • Team ($249): up to 5 Macs across one team or organization.
  • Enterprise: volume seats, annual contract, custom deployment.

You may transfer a license to another Mac by deactivating the old one from the app's Settings. If you change Macs, deactivate the old Mac in Settings → Licence and activate on the new one. No re-purchase needed.
