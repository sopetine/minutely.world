How it works

Before, during, after.

Minutely runs in the background — you stay focused on the conversation. Here's what happens before, during, and after each call.

01 · BEFORE

Detects your meetings

Minutely reads your calendar and gets ready the moment a Teams, Zoom, or Google Meet call starts. You never click record.

02 · DURING

Transcribes live

Whisper runs on your Mac's Neural Engine. Words appear with speaker labels in real time — nothing leaves your device.

03 · AFTER

Summarises everything

A short summary, chapter markers, and action items appear the moment the call ends. Every past meeting is searchable from the same place.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Where does my audio go?
Nowhere, by default. Transcription, summaries, and search all run on your Mac. The only thing that leaves your device is what you explicitly send to a cloud AI provider you set up yourself.
Does Minutely need a bot to join my calls?
No. Minutely runs entirely on your Mac and starts capturing the meeting's audio the moment you join a supported call. There is no separate participant bot.
Which platforms are supported?
Microsoft Teams (including the new Teams 2.0), Zoom, and Google Meet. Anything that runs on macOS 13 or later works — Intel and Apple Silicon.
Can I use my own AI model?
Yes. Bring your own OpenAI-compatible API key in Settings, or pick a local LLM (Llama, Mistral, Phi) for fully offline summaries. Both work with the same recordings.
Is this a subscription?
No. Every tier is a single one-time purchase. All future updates are included — Minutely will never charge you again for the same Mac.
I need more seats later — what do I do?
Email us and we'll set you up — no need to repurchase. You only ever pay for the difference when you add seats.
