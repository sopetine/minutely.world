FAQ

Quick answers.

Still have questions? Email hello@minutely.app — a real person replies within a day.

Does Minutely need a bot to join my calls?
No — Minutely runs on your Mac and captures audio locally the moment you join a supported call. No "Minutely Notetaker" shows up for anyone else.
Where does my audio and transcript data go?
Nowhere, by default. Transcription and (optionally) summarization can run fully on-device with a local model. If you bring your own API key, those calls go to your chosen provider — never to us.
Which platforms are supported?
Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Google Meet — fully supported on day one. We're adding Webex and Slack huddles based on demand.
Can I use my own AI model?
Yes. Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint — OpenAI, Anthropic (via proxy), Azure, OpenRouter, or local Ollama and LM Studio. Set the base URL, paste the key, choose the model.
Is this a subscription?
No. Every tier is a one-time purchase. Free updates are included forever.
Will this drain my battery or slow my Mac?
Minutely is designed to use less than 5% CPU on Apple Silicon during a meeting. Battery impact is comparable to running a chat app in the background.
