Quick answers.

Where does my audio go?
Nowhere, by default. Transcription, summaries, and search all run on your Mac. The only thing that can ever leave your device is transcript text you choose to send to an AI provider, with your own key. Audio never leaves.
Does Minutely need a bot to join my calls?
No. Minutely runs entirely on your Mac and starts capturing the meeting's audio the moment you join a supported call. There is no separate participant bot.
Which platforms are supported?
Microsoft Teams (including the new Teams 2.0), Zoom, and Google Meet — plus any window, your whole screen, or a video file. Minutely runs on Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 13 or later.
Can I use my own AI model?
Yes. Bring your own OpenAI-compatible API key in Settings, or pick a local LLM (Llama, Mistral, Phi) for fully offline summaries. Both work with the same recordings.
Is this a subscription?
No. Every tier is a single one-time purchase. All future updates are included — Minutely will never charge you again for the same Mac.
